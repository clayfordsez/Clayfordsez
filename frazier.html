01 Condition on Arrival

Cabinets rough but intact; sound phenomenal already, straight out of the box.
Seller confirmed via message before purchase: all crossover caps replaced a few years ago, speakers used daily until about two months before the sale, all drivers work. Terminal posts loose in the wood and some plastic missing at the terminal cup — cosmetic/mechanical annoyance, still makes good contact.
Serial numbers: No. 55895 and No. 55878.
Learned from a Frazier Users Group admin that the rear-panel level-control pots were a dealer-requested sales feature, not part of the factory design — wide open (pots removed/bypassed) is the actual factory spec, and many owners remove them entirely. On at least one of this pair, the pots have already been disconnected and removed — confirmed by two blank bolt holes visible in the seller's listing photos where they used to mount.

02 Known Issues

Tweeter buzz undiagnosed

Most obvious during the crescendo in "How to Disappear Completely" by Radiohead.

Runaway woofer undiagnosed

One woofer resonates uncontrollably once volume passes a certain threshold — the oscillation escalates once it starts, to the point of needing to physically get up and cut the volume.
Since the seller's caps are recent, a fresh recap probably isn't the fix here — this points toward a driver or mechanical issue rather than aged crossover components. Plan is to open up the problem woofer directly once bandwidth allows.

03 Factory Spec (on file)

Low frequency: 12" driver, 8lb Alnico V magnet, 2.5" voice coil.
Mid: 8" full-range driver, isolated in its own sealed sub-enclosure.
High: 3×7 compression horn + direct-coupled piezo super horn.
Crossover: series-parallel, 12dB/octave, air-core inductors (#15 copper wire), polycarbonate + metalized mylar caps. Crossover points at 600Hz and 3000Hz–4000Hz.
Cabinet: 29.25" × 25.75" × 16", rear-ported ("tuning slots"), intended to sit 1–3" from a reflective surface behind them.
Per a Frazier Users Group contributor, the version with one piezo tweeter and one 3×7 horn uses a 3.0mH coil paired with a 24µF cap, and a 0.39mH coil paired with a 3µF cap.
For reference, the seller's own recapped pair used two "PB-MKP-FC" film caps (24µF and ~6.2µF, both 400V, made in France) mounted on a Frazier-branded crossover box directly on the woofer basket, alongside an air-core coil.

04 Open Items

  • Open up and diagnose the runaway/resonating woofer
  • Source a parts supplier for the drivers, in case replacement becomes necessary (asked the Frazier Users Group, no answer yet)
  • Decide whether to reinstall level-control pots or leave the design in its factory wide-open state
