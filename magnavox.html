Diagnosis Project Phases Parts Status

01 Diagnosis

Current crossover has a lot of overlap between the horn and woofer, causing a honky character in the sound.
This project doubles as a teaching exercise — a low-stakes way to learn crossover/L-pad tuning by ear, with decent-sounding speakers as a bonus outcome rather than the primary goal.

02 Project Phases

Big Juicy 1.1 in progress

Cap swap on the existing Magnavox speakers, plus an L-pad — the fast, low-commitment first phase.
Will not add a woofer inductor in this phase — just the series cap on the horn and the L-pad. Woofer stays full-range for now.

Big Juicy 1.2 under consideration

Possible next phase: adding a woofer inductor. This step would need actual measurement (mic + REW) rather than by-ear tuning, since it's a harder change to judge subjectively.

Big Juicy II concept only

A from-scratch rebuild — still pure concept, no drivers purchased and no commitment yet to reusing the Magnavox cabinets themselves.
Rapidly evolving into a full custom build centered on sourcing genuinely nice-looking vintage cabinets, rather than just any cheap donor cabinet.

03 Parts Status

Madisound order (4µF Solen cap) — delivered.
Parts Express order (2.2µF / 2.7µF / 3.3µF caps + L-pad) — delivered.
All parts needed for the 1.1 listen-testing phase are in hand. Currently queued behind other projects — eager to get to the L-pad/cap testing once bandwidth opens up.
